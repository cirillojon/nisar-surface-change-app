**This app does none of these today.** It shows about a year of mostly provisional data at a few places. Each row below is something that record points toward, and what would have to be added first.

NISAR's orbit repeats every 12 days. That is not a guarantee of two acquisitions at every location in each repeat: local coverage depends on the track, acquisition plan and viewing direction. The mission carries NASA's L-band radar (~24 cm) and ISRO's S-band radar (~9.4 cm, mainly over India); this app currently processes LSAR data only. Its data are free.

| Application | Relies on | What it would take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flood mapping under cloud** | Calm open water reads dark in HH | Faster delivery; a classifier for wind-roughened water and water under vegetation |
| **Forest-clearing alerts in the cloudy tropics** | HV drops where trees are removed | HV on every pass and a stable baseline |
| **Harvest timing and sown area** | HV follows canopy growth and removal | Field boundaries, crop maps, several seasons |
| **Soil moisture** | SME2 retrieves volumetric soil moisture on a 200 m grid (accuracy goal 0.06 m³/m³ where vegetation is light) | Apply retrieval-quality flags; validation where vegetation is dense |
| **Subsidence and aquifer management** | Millimetre-scale motion from interferometry | Both viewing directions, GNSS ties, an atmospheric correction |
| **Roads, levees and rail lines** | Motion along a line at every pass | A reference that is known to be stable; validation on the ground |
| **Landslides** | Brightness change after a failure; slow creep in phase | Both viewing directions, to see into shadowed slopes |
| **Volcanoes** | New lava in brightness; swelling in phase | Interferometry on every pass, fast delivery |
| **Glaciers and ice sheets** | Fronts in brightness; speed from pixel offsets | Offsets on every pair of passes; a longer record |
| **Permafrost** | Seasonal heave and settling of thawing ground | Several years of interferometry |
| **Rivers and coasts** | Water and land edges in HH | Passes at the same water level, compared year to year |
