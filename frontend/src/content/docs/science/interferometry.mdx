NISAR records where in its wave cycle each echo returns (the phase). If the ground moves between passes, the phase shifts. At L-band, one full phase cycle corresponds to about half a wavelength, or 12 cm, of line-of-sight displacement. A GUNW contains unwrapped interferometric phase and ancillary layers; displacement is inferred after referencing and correction, not stored as a direct 3-D ground-motion vector.

- **Line of sight**: motion is measured along the radar's slanted view. + is toward the satellite, − away.
- **Relative**: values are against a reference point assumed stable. In [Mexico City](/workspace?site=mexico-city-subsidence) it's Ciudad Universitaria.

## Errors

| Source | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Troposphere (water vapour) | Delays that vary by date |
| Ionosphere | Large smooth delays, strong at L-band; GUNW supplies a phase screen that this app subtracts |
| Decorrelation | Noise where the surface changed (lava, ploughing) |
| Unwrapping | Jumps of ~12 cm |

Site series and **Draw line** use the ionosphere-screen correction by default, not a correction set selected for making the time series look smooth. Draw-line results show scatter under four correction alternatives as a sensitivity diagnostic. Tropospheric correction remains a limitation, and individual steps can be noisy. The current phase-to-LOS polarity follows the app's NISAR product interpretation (+ toward, − away); the Handbook's general phase equation alone does not establish a product-specific sign, so sign should be checked against independent displacement references.
