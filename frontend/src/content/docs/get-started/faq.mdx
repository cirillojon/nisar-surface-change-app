## Limits

- **Short, provisional record.** 10–29 passes per site from 2025–26, mostly provisional granules. Values may shift when NASA reprocesses them.
- **One number per box.** Site values are medians or shares over the whole box, which mixes fields, towns and water.
- **Open water is one threshold** (HH below −18 dB). It misses wind-roughened water and water under vegetation, which looks bright. At farm sites the same measure is called "very dark pixels", since smooth bare ground can be as dark.
- **Displacement is relative and slanted.** Measured toward or away from the satellite, against a reference point, with no tropospheric correction. Trust trends over months, not single steps.
- **Coverage varies.** Dates with under 60% of the box valid are skipped; under 95% are drawn hollow.
- **No causes.** Rain and river flow are shown on the same dates. Nothing is tested.
- **Related series are models**, for one grid cell. Two rain models differ by 10–30%.
- **Sites were selected** because their series showed a clear change.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?** No. Downloading raw NISAR files needs a free Earthdata Login.

**Why does my area have few passes?** Processing is still catching up. Widen the dates or try another product.

**Why are images grainy?** Speckle, normal in radar images.

**Is this a NASA product?** No. It's an open-source Space Apps 2026 project. NISAR data: NASA/JPL-Caltech and ISRO, via the ASF DAAC.

**Found a mistake?** Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/cirillojon/nisar-surface-change-app).
