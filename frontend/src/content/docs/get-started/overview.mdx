## Try it

1. **[Sylhet](/workspace?site=sylhet-haor-floods)**: press play. Dark areas are calm open water: 53% of the box on 14 July, 14% on 11 November.
2. **[Mexico City](/workspace?site=mexico-city-subsidence)**: 204 mm of motion away from the satellite in a year.
3. **Draw area**: drag a rectangle anywhere and step through every NISAR pass over it.

**Tour** in the map toolbar walks through the controls at Kīlauea.

## Data

- NISAR values are computed from Level-2 and Level-3 granules from NASA's ASF DAAC. Each value names its granule.
- Rain and river flow are public records, shown on the same dates for comparison only.
- NISAR launched on 30 July 2025, so records are under a year long and mostly **provisional**. See [Limits & FAQ](/docs/get-started/faq).
